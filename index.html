Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Why is your estimate higher than the other one?

Usually because ours lists the preparation and theirs does not. Ask the cheaper quote what filling, sanding, caulking and priming is included — the answer explains the difference.

Can you just paint over this water stain?

Not until we know what caused it. Coating over active moisture fails within a season, and then it looks like the paint was the problem. We will tell you if a leak needs another trade first.

My house was built in 1965. Does that matter?

Yes. Work disturbing paint in pre-1978 properties falls under the EPA lead-safe rules — contained work areas, specific practices and an information pamphlet before we start. Any firm that ignores that is cutting a legal corner.

Why did my painted cabinets peel?

Almost always grease. Cooking residue cannot be primed over, so degreasing and sanding is the real work. It is the most common failure in cabinet painting.

Can you paint the exterior this week?

Only if the weather allows it. Products have temperature and humidity limits on the data sheet, and applying outside them is how a coating fails early. We will reschedule rather than rush it.

Do you guarantee the paint will last?

We stand behind our workmanship on the terms in the estimate. We cannot warranty against substrate moisture, structural movement or someone else's work, and we will say which is which before starting.
