Program 03 · Degreased, or it peels

Cabinet & trim finishing

Kitchen cabinets carry cooking grease that no primer will bond through, so degreasing and sanding is the whole job. Painted cabinets that peel at the handles within a year were sprayed over grease — it is the single most common failure in this work.
